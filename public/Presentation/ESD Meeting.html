📌 Makatu Business Growth Highlights

IT Advancements

Our systems have strengthened significantly, achieving 100% on-time POD uploads on the BMW interface. The Ford auto-update integration is in its final stage, syncing seamlessly with the central processing server for real-time data flow.

Fleet Expansion

We have procured MAK009, increasing our capacity for handling vehicle logistics. This new carrier will support long-haul deliveries and assist in covering our expanding operational footprint.

Expanding Footprint

New routes have been launched in Port Elizabeth (PE) and Cape Town (CPT). We have also signed a renewal contract for Ford Remarketing, enabling us to provide services for all Gauteng dealer deliveries, including existing shuttle moves.

Record Performance

We achieved a record-breaking invoice total of R2.1 Million for November 2024, marking our highest revenue month to date. This milestone is a testament to our growing efficiency and business expansion.

BMW KPI Excellence

Our commitment to service quality has resulted in an outstanding 99.36% on-time delivery rate for BMW, ensuring reliability and high performance across our operations.

Security & Compliance

We are among the first logistics providers to achieve top-tier compliance in the industry. Our adherence to stringent security measures positions us at the forefront of safe and efficient vehicle transport.

📌 Operational Improvements & Innovations

We have optimized our real-time tracking and reporting system, ensuring 100% on-time POD uploads for BMW. Ford auto-updates via the central processing server are now in the final integration stage.
