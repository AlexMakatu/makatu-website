Why We Tendered

Our operations are built for adaptability. We tendered for all three models not just to qualify — but to demonstrate our readiness, versatility, and ability to serve BMW's evolving distribution strategy no matter the structure chosen.

Scenario 1: Traditional Transport

BMW assigns all routes and schedules — we execute with zero deviation. It’s a strict, command-driven model where performance, not planning, defines value.

We’ve proven our strength in this space: 0 damages across 1,581 deliveries and an average on-time rate of 99.68% in 2025 YTD. EDI is live, EPOD works flawlessly, and we’ve never missed a compliance checkpoint.

Our growing fleet — including 3 new 11-car carriers and two rapid-response single carriers — gives us national readiness, but Gauteng remains our anchor. We already serve 70% of its dealers with near-perfect scores.

➤ We don’t just handle “traditional” — we dominate it.

📍

Scenario 2: Region-Based Operation

Makatu is positioned to lead regional logistics from BMW’s key hubs: Rosslyn and Durban. With 70% of Gauteng’s BMW dealer network already under our wing, our systems and fleet are fully primed to scale nationally.

We've launched our new N3 route with a dedicated 9-car carrier and are finalising our removal-of-goods license — which will enable bonded vehicle movements from Durban to Gauteng under customs control. This is crucial to support BMW’s import/export requirements and provides a direct pipeline to inland markets.

Our RTMS certification is underway, unlocking PBS approval for our expanding 11-car carrier fleet. This gives us the regulatory muscle to optimize coastal, long-haul, and inland regional deliveries from DRB and Rosslyn alike.

➤ We’re already aligned to BMW’s hub-and-spoke model — and ready to lead regional routing from both ends of the country.

🧩

Scenario 3: General Contractor Model

Though we're confident we can deliver in all models, we propose Option 3 — the General Contractor model — as the most optimized and agile experience for BMW and all LSPs involved.

This structure enables centralized coordination, full visibility, and real-time responsiveness through our Ride platform. If capacity issues arise in specific regions, we can seamlessly activate or request BMW-approved LSPs through our integrated system — ensuring zero delay in fulfillment and transparency across every move.

Our platform is ready for this. The process aligns directly with BMW’s tender objectives and avoids sub-subcontracting concerns while offering adaptive scale.

➤ We believe Option 3 is the highest-value route to performance, visibility, and collaboration. Built to scale, built for BMW.
