📌 Makatu Delivery Model

Receiving Instruction

Delivery instructions can be received through email, portal uploads, or BMW EDI. Once received, stockcontroller is notified and stock is instantly available in the system for staff to access and verify.

Planning the Instruction

At this stage, we assess the earliest available carrier and prioritize shipments based on age and type. Our system ensures that time-sensitive deliveries move first, optimizing efficiency and minimizing delays.

Vehicle will also be planned on the BMW system for yard operater to be able to get vehicle ready for collection

Pre-Loading Inspection

Before loading, a thorough collection assessment ensures that every vehicle is received in pristine condition. High-mileage and long-distance transports are identified, with protective bubble wrap applied where necessary to minimize transit damage.

Loading

Once the driver collects the vehicle, the delivery point is instantly notified with the load details and estimated arrival time. From this stage, the delivery point gains real-time access to track the vehicle’s status and location via the portal.

Delivery of Vehicle

Upon delivery, the dealer receives the Proof of Delivery (POD) via email, and BMW EDI is updated if required. The POD is also accessible to both the dealer and OEM through our secure Dealer Portal, ensuring seamless documentation and tracking.

After Delivery Services and Customer Care

Upon delivery, dealers can report any damages directly, triggering an immediate notification to our Customer Care team for swift claims processing. All damages and claims are logged and made available on our Dealer Portal for full transparency and efficient resolution.
